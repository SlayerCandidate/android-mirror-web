FAQ

Questions people ask.

Does it require an app on my phone?

No. It works with Android’s built-in developer tools and a local USB debug connection.

Is it private?

Yes. The mirror session runs locally on your machine instead of through a remote cloud.

Can I record the screen?

Yes. Recording is built in for demos, QA, and troubleshooting workflows.
